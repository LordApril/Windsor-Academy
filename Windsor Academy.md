## Good questions

Who can enroll?

Only students of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, using their BRAC G-Suite email.

Who teaches the lessons?

BRAC University seniors who completed their thesis and won Thesis of the Year.

Can I share my login?

No. Each login works on one device and one account is allowed per student.

What is the difference between the plans?

Starter gives all 8 chapters, resources and a certificate. Full Access adds live Q&A sessions with mentors.

How can I pay?

bKash or BRAC Bank transfer. Your login is created after we verify payment.
